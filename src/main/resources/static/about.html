About Us

Prayag Institute of Technology and Management

A leading fast-growing educational group in Prayagraj (U.P.), established in 2011 to provide quality technical education.

🏛 Our Story

PITM was established with the vision of creating accessible and practical technical education in the Prayagraj region. The institute focuses on discipline, structured learning, and future-ready skills for diploma students.

🎯 Mission

To provide quality technical education, develop employable skills, and support students with a guided academic environment that values innovation, integrity, and progress.
